Design principles in this screen
Calm hierarchy. One primary action per screen, with server health always one tap away.
Local-first trust. Listening, privacy, and the terminal/server boundary are visible at the moment they matter.
Progressive disclosure. Rooms and assistant are first-class; diagnostics and provisioning stay available without crowding the home.
